<lumi-cells> without a framework

Plain HTML: one tag, the preset, interactive and src attributes, and declarative element binding through data-lc-*.

1. A scene of HTML bubbles

Every bubble with data-lc-influence lights up the grid under it, data-lc-pulse="click" sends a ring on click and data-lc-lift="hover" lifts pixels on hover. The light color (data-lc-color) of the blue bubbles is lighter than their fill, so they do not blend into their own glow.
